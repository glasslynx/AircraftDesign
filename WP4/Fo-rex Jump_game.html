Fo-rex Gump - run, Rex, run!

A free endless runner game for your browser. Jump over cacti, duck under birds, fight bosses like the Kraken, the Pumpkin King and the Sandworm, collect coins and diamonds, and unlock worlds, pets, hats, trails and skins. Controls: Space or Up to jump, Down to duck, X for slow-motion, Esc to pause. On a phone: tap to jump, hold the left side to duck.
